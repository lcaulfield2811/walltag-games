How to play
Press Space to start a round. Wait until the number on screen becomes 0. The instant you see 0, tap Space once.

  2. Watch the number change randomly.
  3. Do not press early.
  4. When the number becomes 0, tap Space once.
  5. Your time will appear immediately.
  6. Then press Space again to start a fresh round.
